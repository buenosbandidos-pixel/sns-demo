Three things we supply

Software

SCADA and control

Supervisory control for treatment equipment: setpoints, dosing logic, alarms and remote operation. The system runs the process, not just the reporting.

Hardware

Sensors and instrumentation

Field instrumentation sized for the site and the parameters that matter, installed where the change actually happens rather than where access is easiest.

Data

Continuous data and integration

Measurement collected without gaps, stored in a form that survives an audit, and integrated with the operator's existing systems.

Why these three belong together: a sensor without control logic is a reading. Control logic without stored data is an operation nobody can verify afterwards. Sold separately they are components; sold together they are evidence.

Where it is used

Water treatment installations

Dissolved oxygen, dosing and process parameters monitored continuously so that a discharge limit or a reuse class can be demonstrated, not asserted.

Water bodies

Lakes, ponds and bays where a restoration result has to be measured against a documented baseline over years rather than sampled twice. See Smart Lake.

Crediting and compliance reporting

Where a measured change becomes a reportable or issuable unit, the monitoring record is the product. The measurement has to be built for that from the start.

What we do not do

We do not supply the treatment equipment. The oxygenation hardware comes from SansOx Oy. We are the layer that operates it and records what it did.
We do not verify our own results. Where independent attribution is required, it is provided by a third party. A measurement system that grades its own output is not evidence.

Where we sit in the chain

The water is treated, the result is measured, the change becomes evidence. Three companies, one coordinated delivery — and you sign once.

Treats the water

SansOx Oy supplies the OxTube unit that dissolves gas into the flow inside the existing pipe.

Measures the result — you are here

SpringNet Solutions Oy — this company — supplies the sensors, control software and continuous data. It records what changed, and it keeps the record.

Turns it into credit-ready evidence

OceanSite Oy develops carbon and restoration instruments from the documented change.

What this chain does not yet deliver. Issuing a credit requires an approved methodology and a registry to issue it into. Neither has been selected yet; issuance will run through specialised partners once they are agreed for the buyer's market, and the record is built to align with the EU CRCF in the meantime. Until then the chain delivers treatment and the evidence of it — not issued credits. This is stated here rather than discovered by the buyer in the first technical meeting.

The three companies are separately registered. The group has no published parent company or joint trading name yet, and none is claimed on this page.
